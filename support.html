FAQ

Common questions about AURA services.

What is AURA Secure?

AURA Secure is a proposed privacy and security service covering privacy controls, device security, security alerts and lost-device assistance.

What is AURA Care?

AURA Care is AURA's proposed after-sales support and service experience.

How does AURA Exchange work?

Eligible devices can follow an evaluation, exchange and upgrade pathway, subject to the company's applicable terms.

Can I get help after purchasing an AURA phone?

Yes. The AURA service concept is designed around ongoing support rather than ending at the point of purchase.
